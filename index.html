About Me

Practical digital solutions built around business goals.

I combine e-commerce knowledge, technical skills and digital marketing to solve problems that affect visibility, customer experience and sales.

More than just making a store look good.

A successful online store needs more than attractive design. Customers need to discover the store, understand the offer, trust the business and have a smooth path toward purchase.

My work focuses on improving that entire journey — from search visibility and traffic acquisition to product presentation, conversion optimization, retention and the underlying digital experience.

Whether the challenge is a Shopify store that is not converting, an SEO opportunity that is being missed, an advertising campaign that needs improvement or a digital system that needs to be built, I approach the problem from a business perspective.

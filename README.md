My E-commerce Approach

I don't treat an online store as simply a website.

A strong e-commerce experience connects several stages:

Traffic → User Experience → Trust → Product Presentation → Conversion → Retention

My approach is to look at the complete customer journey rather than focusing on one isolated element.

1. Understand

I consider the business, products, target customers, positioning, and commercial objectives.

2. Analyze

I review the available store or website information to identify usability issues, SEO opportunities, conversion barriers, content gaps, trust issues, and customer journey friction.

3. Prioritize

Not every issue needs to be fixed at the same time. I prioritize opportunities according to potential impact and project objectives.

4. Optimize

I contribute improvements across areas such as navigation, product presentation, messaging, SEO structure, trust elements, user experience, and conversion paths.

5. Measure

Where performance data is available, I use relevant metrics and business objectives to understand what is working and what should be improved next.

6. Grow

Once the foundation is stronger, acquisition and retention strategies can be developed or scaled more effectively.
